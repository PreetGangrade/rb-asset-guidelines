# Red Bull Streaming Platform — Content Partner Asset Guidelines

## Register
brand — a scroll-driven marketing/guidelines microsite; the design is the deliverable.

## Users
Content partners (production companies, federations, studios) delivering artwork to the Red Bull Streaming Platform (Red Bull TV, ServusTV On, Rally.TV), plus the internal artwork team who point partners here.

## Purpose
Teach one idea: a single Universal Base Asset (UBA), composed center-weighted, adapts across every canvas the platform renders. Then tell partners exactly what to deliver and how. Success = partners ship compliant, high-quality UBAs on the first attempt.

## Brand personality
Cinematic, confident, precise, with a hand-annotated editorial layer (chalk scribbles over product imagery). Red Bull house voice: direct, energetic, no filler. Copy rule: no em dashes, ever.

## Committed visual identity (identity-preservation wins)
- Bull Text (self-hosted) is the brand face; IBM Plex Mono for spec labels; Caveat for scribbles.
- Navy #000F1E ground with #DB0A40 red accents; white hero; brand tints per destination (rbtv blue, servus orange, rally slate).
- Desktop-first pinned scroll-story: fixed 1440×800 canvases scaled by --hs, GSAP ScrollTrigger scrubs, reduced-motion static fallbacks, sub-900px mobile gate.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS landing pages; template card-grid marketing sites; anything that reads as a slide deck instead of a story.
