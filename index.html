Our approach

Artwork that earns the play.

A viewer decides in seconds. Before a single frame plays, your artwork has already told them what your title is — the world it lives in, who it follows, and how it feels. When the image is honest, people press play and stay. When it misleads, they leave — and your work goes unseen. So every asset we create is accurate first, striking always, and unmistakably yours.

Principle 01

The subject leads

Main athletes and characters take center stage — clearly visible, well lit, never cropped tight. Secondary characters only ever appear in context with the people the story is actually about.

Principle 02

True to the title

Discipline, tone, and stakes should read in every image. We never promise a story the content doesn't deliver — misleading artwork loses viewers faster than no artwork at all.

Principle 03

Diverse, not different

A good suite varies the moment, the emotion, and the setting — while the essence never changes. Ten near-identical frames aren't a suite; ten honest angles on one story are.

The Universal Base Asset

One frame. Every screen.

Everything on the platform is cut from a single master image — the Universal Base Asset. From one UBA we derive every canvas the product needs: billboards, rails, search tiles, mobile cards, and the ten-foot TV experience. Deliver one strong UBA, and your title looks right everywhere — today, and after every redesign to come.

Raw assets & source material

What to send us.

Our artwork team builds every suite from what you provide. The stronger and more flexible the source material, the better your title looks on service. Five things matter most.

Resolution

Highest resolution, always

Uncompressed, straight from the source. Screenshots from playback can't be used — stills must come from the master video itself.

Layers

Layered and editable

Key art as layered PSDs, with text, logos, characters, and backgrounds separated — so one piece of art can become every canvas.

Stills

Center-weighted stills

10–15 diverse moments with the action in the middle of the frame. Different scenes, emotions, and settings — the truest preview of your title.

Logos

Vector title treatments

AI, EPS, or PDF, with font names included, so the title stays sharp at any size. Legible at a glance beats stylized and unreadable.

Rights

Cleared for promotion

Every asset must be usable to promote your title on and off the platform. If something can't be shown, tell us before we build with it.

Handing it over: organize source material in one folder per title, with subfolders by type — key art, stills, logos, photography. Your artwork producer will align on the transfer when your engagement begins.
